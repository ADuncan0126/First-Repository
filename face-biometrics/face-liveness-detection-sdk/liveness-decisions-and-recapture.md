# Liveness Decisions & Recapture

## Liveness Decisions & Recapture

Use the adapter classification and score within an application policy. `REAL` does not replace capture-quality checks. `SPOOF`, an unusable image, or an uncertain result should trigger guided recapture.

Retry capture for lighting, pose, or framing problems. Retry the service only for transient technical failures. Route exhausted recaptures or policy exceptions to manual review. Do not expose raw biometric inputs in diagnostics.
